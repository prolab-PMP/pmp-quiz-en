About PMP Quiz

PMP Quiz (wayexam.com) helps candidates practice PMP-style questions, review explanations, and track weak areas across PMBOK 8, PMBOK 7, Agile, Hybrid, and ECO topics.

What this site provides

PMP Quiz is a focused practice platform for the Project Management Professional (PMP)® exam. It offers a bank of more than 2,250 scenario-based practice questions, each with an explanation that shows why the best answer fits the PMP mindset rather than only stating which option is correct. Questions are organized by category, difficulty, and study mode so you can target the areas where you are weakest.

Free visitors can try a 30-question sample set immediately, with no account required. Registered members can save progress, build a wrong-answer notebook, bookmark questions, and use structured practice and mock-exam modes. Premium access unlocks the full question bank and detailed performance analytics.

How the practice works

The questions follow the style of the current PMP Examination Content Outline (ECO) and reflect both predictive (waterfall) and agile/hybrid ways of working, which together make up the majority of the live exam. Beyond practice questions, our blog publishes free English study guides covering PMBOK 7 vs 8 changes, earned value, change control, risk versus issue, procurement contracts, the People domain, and a 90-day study plan.

Languages

The interface and questions are available in English, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, and Japanese, so candidates preparing in different regions can study in the language they are most comfortable with.
