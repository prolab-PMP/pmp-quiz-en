# Agile vs Predictive PMP Questions

Many PMP questions are not asking you to name a method. They are asking you to notice the environment and choose a response that fits the work.

## Agile clues

Agile scenarios often mention changing priorities, iterative delivery, product owners, empowered teams, frequent feedback, or uncertain requirements. The best answer usually supports collaboration, transparency, and rapid learning.

## Predictive clues

Predictive scenarios often mention defined scope, formal baselines, change control, regulatory constraints, or fixed contractual expectations. The best answer usually respects the approved plan and formal governance.

## Hybrid clues

Hybrid questions mix both patterns. For example, hardware or compliance work may be predictive while software delivery uses agile iterations. In these questions, avoid one-size-fits-all answers and choose the response that fits the specific part of the work.

## A quick clue table

| If the scenario mentions... | Lean toward | Best-answer tendency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed scope, baselines, change control board | Predictive | Follow the approved plan and formal process |
| Product owner, backlog, sprints, frequent change | Agile | Empower the team, re-prioritize, inspect and adapt |
| Compliance hardware + iterative software | Hybrid | Apply the right approach to each part of the work |
| Heavy regulation, audit, contracts | Predictive | Respect governance and documentation |
| Uncertain requirements, fast feedback needed | Agile | Deliver increments, learn, and adjust |

## A worked example

A stakeholder asks for a new feature mid-project. In a predictive scenario with an approved scope baseline, the best first action is usually to submit a change request and let the change control process evaluate it. In an agile scenario, the same request is often handled by adding the item to the product backlog so the product owner can prioritize it for a future iteration. Same request, different correct answer, because the environment is different.

## The most common trap

The most frequent mistake is forcing a method you prefer onto every question. The exam rewards reading the context first. Before answering, name the environment in one word: predictive, agile, or hybrid. Then choose the response that fits that environment.

When reviewing wrong answers, ask which clue you missed. That habit improves your PMP score faster than memorizing lists.
