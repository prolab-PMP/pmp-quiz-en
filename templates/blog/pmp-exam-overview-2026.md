# PMP Exam Overview 2026

The PMP exam tests how well you can apply project management judgment, not just whether you can memorize terminology. A good study plan should combine concept review, scenario practice, and repeated analysis of wrong answers.

## What to focus on

- People and stakeholder scenarios
- Agile, predictive, and hybrid delivery choices
- Risk, issue, quality, and change control decisions
- Value delivery and benefits realization
- Servant leadership and team conflict handling

## How to practice

Start with short 20 to 30 question sets. After each set, review why the correct answer is better than the tempting alternatives. Once accuracy is stable, move into longer mock-exam sessions.

## A simple preparation rhythm

Use the first pass to understand the concepts, the second pass to identify repeated mistakes, and the final pass to build exam stamina. PMP Quiz is designed around that loop: practice, review, save weak areas, and repeat.

## Exam format at a glance

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Questions | 180 scored items |
| Time | 230 minutes, with two scheduled breaks |
| Question types | Multiple choice, multiple response, matching, hotspot, limited fill-in-the-blank |
| Delivery | Test center or online proctored |
| Domains | People, Process, Business Environment |

## Domain weighting

The exam is built around three domains. Knowing the approximate weighting helps you decide where to spend study time.

| Domain | Approximate weight | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People | ~42% | Leading and supporting teams, conflict, stakeholders |
| Process | ~50% | Delivering the work: scope, schedule, risk, quality, change |
| Business Environment | ~8% | Compliance, value, organizational strategy |

About half of the questions reflect predictive approaches and about half reflect agile or hybrid approaches, so you should be comfortable in both worlds.

## Eligibility in one line

To sit the exam you generally need a combination of project management education and documented project experience, plus the required project management training hours. Confirm the current requirements on PMI's official site before you apply, because eligibility rules are set by PMI and can change.
