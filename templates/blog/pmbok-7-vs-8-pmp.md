# PMBOK 7 vs PMBOK 8 for PMP Candidates

PMBOK 7 shifted the focus from process memorization toward principles and performance domains. PMBOK 8 builds on that direction while making it easier to connect principles, lifecycle choices, and practical project delivery work.

## What this means for study

You still need to understand processes, but the exam often asks what a project manager should do next in a real situation. That means your preparation should include both structure and judgment.

## Practical study tips

- Learn the principle behind each answer, not just the answer itself.
- Compare predictive, agile, and hybrid responses for the same scenario.
- Review stakeholder and team questions carefully because wording often changes the best response.
- Use mock exams only after short practice sets expose your weak areas.

The best PMP preparation connects PMBOK knowledge with scenario-based decision making.
